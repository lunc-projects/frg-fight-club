The seed

The opening deposit sets the starting price: SOL on one side, SOLDEV on the other. More seed means a steadier price that is harder to push around; less seed means wilder swings. The number is Tom and Hussy's call.

Liquidity that can't be pulled

Depositing into a pool gives back LP tokens — the receipt that lets you withdraw. Here, 100% of that receipt is burned at launch: sent to Solana's incinerator address, gone forever. Nobody — not the team, not anyone — can ever pull this pool's liquidity.
