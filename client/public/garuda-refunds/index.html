🖼️ The NFT side

On the NFT side nothing has been repaid yet. 1,084 NFTs were taken and 10,642 NFTs are frozen in the contracts awaiting return. The swept NFT reward pools held 319,372,082,398 DO, and the ROTTI cross-staking pool held another 46,712,277,716 DO in rewards. Those amounts are shown here as totals — the per-wallet NFT lists have not been published yet.
