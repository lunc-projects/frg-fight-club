🛠️ Build Tools

Self-serve on-chain building on the Kava Launchpad DEX — your wallet signs there; this page never holds keys. Anything else — bots, games, websites, pages — just ask in the 💬 Chat.

🪙 Create a Token

Launch your own token on Kava EVM through the factory — fixed supply, no mint, no owner, no tax.

  1. Open the DEX and tap the Token Factory tab.
  2. Fill in the name, symbol and supply — creation fee is 10 KAVA.
  3. Check the review sheet, then sign in your wallet.
Open the DEX →

💧 Seed / Add Liquidity

Open a pool or add to one. A brand-new pair is seeded at the ratio you choose — that ratio is the starting price.

  1. Open the DEX and tap the Pools tab.
  2. Pick the pair — KAVA/USDT has its own 🌱 Seed button while it has no pool.
  3. Check the live pool panel and the review sheet, then sign.
Open the DEX →

💸 Withdraw Liquidity

Take your share back out of a pool — you see every token that comes out, and what's left of your position, before you sign.

  1. Open the DEX and tap the Pools tab.
  2. Find your position under Your positions and tap Withdraw.
  3. Pick 25 / 50 / 75 / 100% or type an amount, review, then sign.
Open the DEX →

🥩 Create Staking / Farming

Your own staking pool or LP farm, with pause + emergency withdraw built in. Rewards are funded by you, the creator.

  1. Open the DEX and tap the Staking & Farming tab.
  2. Set the stake token, reward token, reward amount and days — creation fee is 10 KAVA.
  3. Create it, then fund the rewards to start the clock.
Open the DEX →
