CONTROLS

👆 FINGER FOLLOW
Drag anywhere on the field — the Bot flies to a point just above your fingertip, so it never hides under your thumb. Let go and it settles where it is. Your cannons fire on their own. Desktop: mouse drag works the same, or arrow keys / WASD.
⏩ DASH (desktop: Shift, or double-tap a direction key)
to DASH — a split-second burst to dodge a dive, ready again in about a second.
⚡ HOLD YOUR FINGER STILL
Finger down and held still, to charge a massive PLASMA BLAST and release to fire! It pierces everything in its path — the longer the charge, the harder it hits. Desktop: hold Space.

SHIPS

✦
FREE FIGHTERS FLY THE SAME
The four free fighters are cosmetic — same handling, same firepower, scores together on one leaderboard. The NFT fighters (marked NFT) carry real upgrades — faster cannons, extra hull, stronger plasma — and score on their own board.

The tokens wear the real logos of the chain on their faces — the LUNC crescent rides the standard token, the USTC coin rides the gold, the JURIS mark rides the armored, WESO rides the swift, LCM the rug, PIZZAB the splitter, LCW the ghost, and DO the carrier. Deeper in, the sky fights back: the spinning LUNC tokens return fire past 400m, space mines drift down and blow, and alien raiders weave in shooting. Tokens that slip past drain the pool — and you only have three hull shields. Chain kills to light 🔥 FEVER for double points. Clear each level's mission to earn ★ STARS ★. Every level ends with a boss — THE VAULT, or worse, THE RUG WHALE. Beat level 5 to unlock ENDLESS MODE. Hold the line, defender! 🛡
