How to fight

Drag the stick (or ◀ ▶) to move. LIGHT is fast, HEAVY hits hard — land HEAVY up close to THROW your rival. Tap-tap-tap builds combos. Hits charge your SPECIAL meter (2 charges max): unleash the POND BOLT at range. Hold BLOCK to guard — blocked hits barely scratch you, but throws break through. Win 2 rounds to take the match. Keyboard: ←/→ move, J light, K heavy, L block, U special.
