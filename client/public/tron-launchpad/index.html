Launchpad — launch your own token on TRON

Launch a token that trades from minute one

No liquidity pool needed. Your token sells on a bonding curve — a price formula held by a smart contract — until it is ready to graduate to a real DEX.

What is a bonding curve?

A bonding curve is a vending machine for your token, run by a contract. Buyers pay TRX in and get tokens out; sellers do the reverse. The price is set by a formula: the more of the supply has sold, the higher the price climbs. Nobody sets the price by hand, and the contract can never pay out more than it holds.

The road of a launch

  1. Create — you fill in the form: name, supply, starting price, your logo. The full supply is created inside the curve contract. Nothing is pre-minted to any wallet unless you choose a small pre-mint (max 10%) for one wallet you name.
  2. Trade on the curve — anyone can buy and sell against the curve right away. 80% of the supply is for sale on the curve; 20% is held back for the DEX.
  3. Graduation — if you picked a graduation target and the curve's TRX reserve reaches it, trading on the curve freezes and the launch graduates.
  4. On the DEX — the held-back 20% plus the raised TRX seed a real trading pair, and your token trades on the open market from there.

What is the LP, and what happens to it?

LP tokens are the receipt for the money and tokens seeded into the DEX pair. Whoever holds the LP can pull the liquidity back out — so at graduation the LP is minted straight to a burn address that nobody controls. The liquidity can never be pulled by anyone, including you. That is on purpose: your buyers can check it on-chain.

Fees, stated plainly

You pick the trading fee when you create: 1%, 2.5% or 5% of each trade. Of every fee: half goes to the platform, a quarter goes to you as the creator (you claim it from your launch page), and a quarter stays inside the curve as extra backing, which makes every remaining token worth a little more. If your launch graduates, 1% of the seeded TRX goes to the platform and the rest seeds the pair. There are no other fees and no hidden cuts.

Your protection controls

  • 3-second cooldown — every wallet must wait 3 seconds between one operation and the next. Bots cannot fire rapid-fire trades. You can change the wait (up to 1 hour) or set it to 0.
  • Sell limit — cap how many tokens any one sale can move, so a single big wallet cannot crash your price in one hit. You set the cap; 0 means no cap.
  • Blacklist — block a wallet from buying or selling on your curve. Your list, under your control. (Use the Wallet checker tab to look a wallet up first.)
  • Emergency stop & recall — you can pause your launch at any time. In a real emergency, recall sweeps only the funds still inside the contract (unsold tokens and the TRX reserve) to a pre-set safe wallet and shuts the launch down. Tokens already in buyers' wallets are theirs — no function can take them, and that is by design.
  • Anti-snipe — an opening window where each wallet's buys are capped, so one bot cannot eat the cheap start.

Questions

Do I need a liquidity pool or a lot of money to start?

No. The curve is the market at the start. You only need enough TRX in your wallet for the network fees of the creation transaction.

Can more tokens be created later?

No. The supply is fixed at creation and there is no minter — not for you, not for us, not for anyone. Anyone can verify that on-chain.

Who owns my launch?

You do — the wallet that creates it. Pause, blacklist, cooldown, sell limit and recall all answer to your wallet only. This page never holds your keys; every action is signed in your own TronLink.

What if my token never reaches the graduation target?

It simply keeps trading on the curve. Graduation is optional — you can also choose “stay on the curve” when you create, and many tokens do exactly that.
