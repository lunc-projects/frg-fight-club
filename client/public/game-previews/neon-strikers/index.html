NEON STRIKERS

NEON WORLD DOME CUP
★ LUNA CLASSIC ★

Real 3-v-3 arcade football under the dome. Dribble, pass, charge your shot, beat the keeper — win the Quarter-Final, Semi-Final and Final to lift the cup.

How to Play — Arena Rules

🕹️ Move: left joystick / drag, or WASD / arrows. The glowing ring is your active player — it switches to whoever has the ball or is closest on defence.

🟢 PASS / TACKLE: PASS button or J. With the ball it passes to a team-mate; without it, your active player lunges / tackles.

🟣 SHOOT: hold SHOOT or K to charge, release to fire at the top goal. More charge = more power, but keepers dive faster in later rounds.

🏟️ Arena rules: walls keep the ball in play — it rebounds off every boundary except the goal mouths. No throw-ins, no corners: pure flow. Goals reset to a kick-off.

⏱️ Each match is two 40-second halves. Win to advance: QF → SF → Final. Lose and the run ends. Score = 150 per round won + 50 per goal.
