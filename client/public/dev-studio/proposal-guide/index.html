📜 How to Put Up a Proposal

Brand-new contract code can only reach Luna Classic through a governance proposal. This guide walks the whole route in plain words — who needs one, the five steps, and the honest fine print.

Who needs a proposal?

You do — if your project needs brand-new contract code uploaded to the chain: custom contracts the chain has never seen. New code can only be stored on Luna Classic with the stakers' permission, and a proposal is how you ask for it.

✅You do NOT need a proposal for…▾
Tokens and NFTs created through the existing Token Factory · games · websites and bots · normal buying and selling. Those are ordinary transactions — no vote, no deposit, no waiting.

The five steps

1

Write it in plain words

Say what the code is, what it does, and why the chain should accept it. A code upload is a Store Code proposal — the contract code itself travels with the proposal, so anyone can inspect exactly what they are voting on.

2

Submit it from your wallet

Open the governance section of your wallet and create the proposal. You sign it yourself — nobody else can submit it for you, and nobody ever needs your seed phrase or keys to help you with it.

3

Fund the deposit

A proposal needs a minimum deposit of 5,000,000 LUNC before it can reach a vote. You have up to 14 days to reach the full amount, and anyone who supports your project can add to it.

4

The vote — 7 days

With the deposit full, voting opens for 7 days. Stakers vote with their staked LUNC: Yes, No, No with Veto, or Abstain. Your job that week: tell your community the vote is live and answer questions honestly.

5

The result

If it passes, the code is stored on-chain and your project can start building on it. The deposit is returned — with one exception: if more than a third of the vote is No with Veto, the deposit is burned instead. That veto is the chain's spam filter, and it is why step 1 matters.

⚠️Read this before you start▾

Public and permanent. Everything in a proposal lives on-chain, readable by anyone, forever. Write it like you mean it.

Days to weeks, not minutes. Up to 14 days in the deposit period plus a 7-day vote — plan your launch date around it.

The deposit is real money at risk only in the veto case. A proposal that passes or simply fails gets its deposit back; a vetoed one loses it.

Deposit, timing and vote figures checked against the live Luna Classic governance parameters.
