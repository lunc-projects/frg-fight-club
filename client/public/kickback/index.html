How it works

1

Delegate LUNC to LUNC Kickback

Stake your LUNC to the validator from your wallet, or head to luncback.com to get set up.

2

Earn normal staking rewards

Your delegation earns standard Terra Classic staking rewards, just like staking with any validator.

3

Get cashback on top

LUNC Kickback shares its commission back with delegators — you get a share of the monthly reward pool, on top of your staking rewards.
