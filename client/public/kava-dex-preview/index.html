Create Your Own Chain

Your own chain, connected to Kava — in plain words, step by step.

1

Scaffold your chain

Start from the Cosmos SDK with the Ignite CLI — one command lays down a working blockchain: accounts, staking, governance, your token.

2

Run testnet validators

Spin up a testnet with your own validators. Break things safely, tune the settings, invite testers — no real money at risk.

3

Connect to Kava over IBC

Open IBC channels between your chain and Kava. Your token can now travel to Kava and back, the same way ATOM and OSMO do.

4

List your token on this DEX

Once your token reaches Kava, create its pool here and it trades beside KAVA, USD₮ and USDX from day one.

Straight talk

Kava has no official L2 or rollup product. In Cosmos terms, "your own chain on Kava" means a sovereign Cosmos SDK appchain that sits beside Kava and connects over IBC — it is not secured by Kava. You run your own validators and your own security. Kava Rise is the incentive door for builders. Anyone who tells you otherwise is selling something.

What you actually need

  • Servers for your validators (plus backups) — this is your main running cost.
  • A security audit before real money touches the chain.
  • Starting liquidity for your token's pool on the DEX.
  • IBC relayers keeping the channel to Kava alive, 24/7.
  • An explorer and a faucet so people can see and try your chain.
