Privacy Policy — LUNC Dev Wallet Scope

Last updated: November 3, 2026

LUNC Dev Wallet Scope (the "Scope") is a free, read-only wallet lookup tool provided by LUNC Dev Studio, available on the web at https://www.luncscope.com and as an Android app that displays the same website.

The short version

The Scope collects nothing. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no trackers. It cannot connect to your wallet and it never asks for a seed phrase, private key or password.

What the Scope does

You type or paste a public wallet address. The Scope reads public blockchain data for that address — balances, staking, rewards, NFTs and recent transactions — directly from public blockchain services (public LCD/API endpoints for Terra Classic, DO Chain, Cosmos Hub and Osmosis) and displays it to you.

Public blockchain data is public by design: anyone can look up any address. The Scope simply makes it readable.

What is stored, and where

  • On your device only: the Scope saves your language choice in your browser's local storage so the page opens in your language next time. Nothing else is saved.
  • On our servers: nothing. We keep no accounts, no database of addresses you looked up, and no profiles of visitors.
  • Third-party services: to show prices and chain data, your browser/app talks directly to public blockchain APIs and public price services (such as CoinPaprika and CoinGecko). Those services receive your request (including your IP address, as with any website) under their own privacy policies. We do not control them and we receive no data back about you from them.

Permissions

The Android app requests no device permissions beyond what the web view needs to load the website (internet access).

Children

The Scope is a general-audience information tool and collects no data from anyone, including children.
