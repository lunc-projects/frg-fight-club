🛡️ Safe by design

This console holds no keys and signs nothing. It only points you to the right place and shows live chain data. Building happens on the DEX pages, where your own wallet signs every step.

🔨 Build

🪙 Create a Token

Launch your own token on Kava in about a minute. Fixed supply, minted straight to your wallet — no mint, no owner, no tax. Creation fee: 10 KAVA.

  1. Tap Open the DEX below.
  2. Tap Token Factory at the top.
  3. Type a name, ticker and supply — review it, then your wallet signs.
Open the DEX →

💧 Seed / Add Liquidity

Put a token into a pool so people can trade it. For a brand-new pair — like KAVA/USDT — the amounts you put in set the starting price.

  1. Tap Open the DEX below.
  2. Tap Pools at the top.
  3. Tap + Add Liquidity, pick the pair, type both amounts — you hold the LP tokens and can withdraw any time.
Open the DEX →

🔁 Swap

Trade one token for another at the live pool price. Native KAVA works everywhere — no manual wrapping.

  1. Tap Open the DEX below.
  2. Stay on the Swap tab.
  3. Pick the tokens, type the amount, check the review screen — then your wallet signs.
Open the DEX →

🥩 Create Staking / Farming

Make your own staking pool or LP farm for your token, for a creation fee. You fund the rewards; holders stake and earn. Pause + emergency withdraw built in.

  1. Tap Open the DEX below.
  2. Tap Staking at the top.
  3. Pick the stake token and reward token, set the reward amount and days — review, then your wallet signs.
Open the DEX →
