      ✅ Before You Start — Checklist

      Everything the studio needs from you, in one place. You keep your wallet, seed phrase and project ownership at all times.

      1 · Project details

      • Project name, and what it does in 2–3 sentences
      • Website and social links
      • Main contact: email, Telegram @, or X @
      • Token/NFT contract address, if one already exists
      • The build or package you selected, and anything it must — or must NOT — include

      2 · Art direction

      • Final production artwork is created by the studio
      • Send reference images, rough templates or sketches that show the style you like — direction only, never copied or traced
      • Your preferred colours and the mood: serious, playful, futuristic, classic…
      • Art size: SMALL (logo/icon help, a few key images) · MEDIUM (a complete set for one game or app) · LARGE (full original art direction from zero)
      • An official logo you own or control can be placed as supplied; the surrounding artwork is studio-created

      3 · Wallet & ownership

      • Choose the project wallet that will own the finished build
      • Provide the public wallet address only
      • NEVER SEND: seed phrase, private key, wallet password, or a photo of your recovery words
      • If a bot is included, you create it through BotFather when we reach that step; any bot token goes only through the studio's secure form

      4 · Payment choice

      • Pick your quote currency (LUNC or USD) and preferred payment method in the Payment & wallet box
      • Only methods marked READY on your final build sheet can be used — a method marked UNDER DEVELOPMENT cannot be used yet
      • Check the asset, amount, destination wallet and order reference carefully before paying
      • Use the exact payment reference or memo on the build sheet, and keep the transaction hash
      • Never pay from an exchange unless the build sheet specifically says exchange payment is accepted

      5 · Review & approval

      • Review and approve the first art direction
      • Review the written build sheet and the final price
      • Test the private preview on your own phone or computer
      • Nothing is called finished until the studio's checks pass AND you approve the version you tested

      ⚠️ Warning labels

      • Store approval is decided by Google or Apple — never guaranteed
      • No exchange/DEX listing, return, reward rate, sales result or token price is ever guaranteed
      • A build marked NEEDS GOVERNANCE PROPOSAL cannot be completed until governance approves it
      • A feature or payment method marked UNDER DEVELOPMENT is not available yet
      • The studio never takes custody of your wallet, seed phrase or project funds

      ✅ What We Can & Can't Do

      The honest version — the same rules we run on our own projects.

      ✅ We can

      • Proven templates — every build on the menu already runs live with real money.
      • Done-For-You — hand us the idea and your public wallet address; we handle design, art, setup, build and testing, and hand it over.
      • Token Factory — create your own CW20 token, minted to your wallet; you're the owner from the first block.
      • Free automated tools — live stats pages, burn counters and safety checkups, generated on the spot from chain data.
      • No custody — your keys, your wallet, your funds. Holders keep their tokens in Hold & Earn.
      • Verified delivery — every deployment is checked against the live chain before it's called done.
      • Governance route — need new contract code? We prepare the full proposal pack for you to submit.

      ✕ We can't

      • No instant new code — since proposal 12234, new contract code uploads need a governance vote. The governance-proposal route exists and we prepare the full pack — but approval isn't ours to promise.
      • No custody, ever — we never hold seed phrases, treasuries, or user funds.
      • No guaranteed returns or listings — no promised APYs, sales, or exchange listings. Estimates are labeled estimates.
      • No gambling mechanics — no paid-entry, house-banked prize games.
      • Luna Classic first — it's the chain we can verify end to end. Other chains come later.
