⚔️ The three attacks — what the blockchain shows

Oct 20, 2026 — NFT staking: 35 contracts, 209 stakers. 1,084 NFTs stolen, 10,642 frozen. Reward pools swept: 692,481,412,955.52 tokens (mixed).

Oct 21, 2026 — Cross-staking: 9 contracts, 446 wallet records — every one listed below, wallet by wallet.

Oct 31, 2026 — Regular pools: 12 pools migrated to drain code. The DO pool alone held 42,169,948,424,016.19 DO in claims from 363 wallets — listed below. The attacker dumped that DO and realized 83,066,759.86 LUNC.

DO taken across all three attacks: 42,998,000,153,789.76 DO (about 43 trillion). The cash value of everything taken in all three attacks was roughly $6,000 — most of it had no real market.

🖼️ The NFT side

On the NFT side nothing has been repaid yet. 1,084 NFTs were taken and 10,642 NFTs are frozen in the contracts awaiting return. The swept NFT reward pools held 319,372,082,398 DO, and the ROTTI cross-staking pool held another 46,712,277,716 DO in rewards.
