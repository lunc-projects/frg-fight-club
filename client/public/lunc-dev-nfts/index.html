Good to know

  • Your collection is 100% yours: you are its creator, minter and admin. LUNC Dev Studio holds no keys and cannot move your NFTs or your money.
  • Collections are standard CW721 — they work with Terra Classic wallets and marketplaces, and your NFTs can always be transferred freely.
  • Creating costs the network fee plus a 100,000 LUNC creation fee, paid to LUNC Dev Studio in the same transaction.
  • Always check the transaction in your wallet before you sign.
