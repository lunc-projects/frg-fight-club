Honest limits

  • Generation happens entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere until you mint.
  • In this phase every token is minted with its own transaction — your wallet will ask you to sign once per token.
  • Minted images are stored through the launchpad's image service so wallets and marketplaces can display them; oversized images are shrunk to fit.
  • Generation is capped at 100 tokens in this phase, and a combination is never repeated inside one generated set.
