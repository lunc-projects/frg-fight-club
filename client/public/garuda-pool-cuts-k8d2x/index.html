🦅 Garuda Pools — Owner Config Cuts

One job: set each of your Garuda staking pools to APR 0 with a 1-day unstake wait — the fastest exit these contracts allow. You sign every step yourself in your wallet app (Galaxy Station or Keplr). This page holds no keys, asks for no seed, and signs nothing on any server.

⚠️ What this does — and what it does not do

These are YOUR pools on Garuda. This sets each pool's rewards to zero and its unstake wait to the 1-day minimum, so your stakers can exit as fast as these contracts allow. It does NOT remove the pool and cannot stop a migration by Garuda's contract admin — warn your stakers to unstake now. Whether the shorter wait applies to unstakes already running is not confirmed; assume it applies to new unstakes.

  • Each pool can only be signed by its own owner wallet — it is the only wallet the contract will accept for a config change. Each card names the wallet it needs.
  • Your seed phrase must never be shared — not with a developer, not in a chat, not typed into any website. This page never asks for it. Anyone who does is a scammer.
