How it works

  1. Get a seat. You start with $5 of tokens, free.
  2. Tell it what you want — a game, a website, an app or a bot. It builds it in front of you.
  3. Builds burn tokens as they build: a page 1M · a game 2M · a tool 1M · a guide 500K. Questions are free.
  4. Seat empty? Refill for $5, paid in LUNC or USTC.
  5. Everything you build is yours — published live in the app.

The token math — 100 users

Every seat is capped at 120M tokens. Nobody can burn more than their seat holds — when it's empty, building stops until they refill.

Your supply behind it: 480M a week from your plan, plus 1.7B banked. Top-ups if it ever gets big: $10 = 500M · $25 = 2B · $50 = 5B · $100 = 12B.

A $5 refill brings in more than those tokens cost you to replace at the $100 top-up rate — refills earn more than the tokens cost.

And your owner view shows every seat's balance live, so you see a heavy user coming long before it could hurt.
