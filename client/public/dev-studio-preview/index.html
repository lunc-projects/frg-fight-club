🛡️ ORBIT DEFENDER — HOW IT WORKS ▾TAP TO OPEN

You fly a starfighter defending the pool. Your finger steers the ship, crystal cannons fire, and you can dash or hold to charge a plasma shot. Waves of token ships and fighter planes come at you, and every level ends with a big Vault ship you have to crack. There are 10 levels, each harder than the last — finish all 10 and Endless mode opens up. It runs right in the browser, phone or computer, nothing to install.

The fighters

  • 4 free fighters, unlocked by how far you fly
  • 6 NFT fighters you can buy inside the game with LUNC. The NFT mints straight to your own wallet, and owning it unlocks that fighter with real advantages — faster, tougher, stronger guns. The game reads ownership from the blockchain, so your fighters are really yours.

Continue

Lose your ship and you get one Continue per run — 10,000 LUNC puts you right back where you went down, score intact.

The boards

Two leaderboards: one for free players, one for NFT players. Your name, your best score and your rank show on the title screen and when your run ends. The title screen also keeps a running count of LUNC burned by the game wallet.
