🐸 FRG — Admin Rotation

One job: move admin of two FRG contracts from your old wallet to your new wallet. You sign every step yourself in your wallet app (Galaxy Station or Keplr). This page holds no keys, asks for no seed, and signs nothing on any server.

⚠️ Read before you sign

  • Admin transfer is permanent. Once it is done, only the new wallet controls these contracts.
  • Double-check the new address character by character before you sign — a transfer to a wrong address cannot be undone.
  • Your new wallet's seed phrase must never be shared — not with a developer, not in a chat, not typed into any website. This page never asks for it. Anyone who does is a scammer.
  • You must connect the old admin wallet to sign — it is the only wallet the chain will accept for this transfer.
