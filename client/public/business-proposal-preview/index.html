01 · The Product

01.The
Product

Abyssal Vault is a deep-sea dive game built for phones and Telegram. You steer a salvage diver down into a neon trench. The deeper you go, the more relics you haul up — but your air runs out the whole way down. Every run is one decision: turn back and bank it, or push deeper and risk the lot.

  • Drag to swim — one finger, nothing to learn
  • Oxygen clock — depth pays more, air costs more
  • Relic haul × depth multiplier — greed is the difficulty setting
  • One-minute runs — built for leaderboards and rematches

02 · Positioning

02.Not Another
Copy

◆One owner per world

Nobody else can field this look. Your players will never find the same game wearing another token’s colours — your version is the only one in the wild.

◆Built for rivalries

The greed-vs-air finish makes last-second losses your players want to share — and avenge. Every near-miss is a reason to run it back and post the score.

◆A daily habit

Boards reset weekly and a run takes a minute. That’s a daily touchpoint with your token in their routine, not a one-off announcement they scroll past.

◆Above the free tier

Free mini-games are the taste. This is the designed edition — poster-grade art, your branding throughout, your own shop and your own books.

03 · The Market

03.Your Community,
On-Chain

Your holders already live in chat, every day. A game turns that energy toward your token — played in your name, scored on your boards, instead of drifting to whoever launched something this week.

  • A weekly event without organising one — leaderboard seasons run on a rhythm: countdown, competition, winner, repeat
  • Something real to sell — in-game NFT items your players actually use, bought from their own wallets, in the open
  • Something real to own — items and scores tied to your project, not rented attention on someone else’s platform

◆Straight talk

A game does not guarantee price action, listings or new buyers — anyone who promises that is selling you a story. What it does guarantee is a working product, owned by you, that gives your community a reason to show up every day.

04 · Application

How You’d
Run It

01.Launch Week Tournament

Open with a seven-day board. Top scores take prizes you set and pay yourself — the studio never touches prize funds. Launch content writes itself: standings posts, daily leaders, a final winner.

02.Weekly Seasons

The board resets every week, so every week starts level. Each season is a countdown, a rivalry and an announcement — a content rhythm your project runs on its own.

03.NFT Drops

Release divers, gear and skins as NFTs in your own shop, on your schedule. Sales land in your wallet and are written into your books automatically.
