How it works

Every scan, the bot reads the real USD market for the top coins and checks two indicators — MACD (12, 26, 9) and DeMarker (7) — on 1-minute candles. A coin counts as a buy only when both point up. The meter on each coin is the strength of that reading, 0–100: under 40 is high risk, 40–69 is watch, and 70 or more — past the white BUY line — is the best opportunity. The single strongest 70+ coin becomes the 🏆 BEST BUY NOW; the rest of the 70+ coins are listed under it. If nothing clears 70, the bot says NO TRADE — a pick that fires every minute is a coin flip, not a signal.

When a call fires, its card shows a trade plan: the entry, a stop-loss and two take-profit prices worked out from the last 14 minutes of price movement (ATR) — the stop sits 1.5× that movement below the entry, the targets 1.5× and 3× above. Planning levels, not guarantees.

This page only ever points up — buys and no-trades. It never tells you to sell short.

A coin whose real candle feed cannot be read is shown UNAVAILABLE — the bot never guesses a price or a signal for it.
