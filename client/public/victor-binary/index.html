How it works — the honest version

Pick a trade length — 1 minute or 5 minutes — and the scanner reads the real Forex market for all pairs above, checking two indicators on candles of that length: MACD (12, 26, 9) and DeMarker (7). A pair only gets a BUY or SELL signal when both indicators point the same way and the signal strength clears 70/100. Anything weaker, or any disagreement, is NO TRADE. The strength number is a measure of the indicator reading — it is not a win probability and not a "confidence" score. The 5-minute candles are built from the same real 1-minute market data.

There is no 5-second button, on purpose. At 5 seconds there is no pattern to read. Apps that sell 5-second signals with a confidence percentage are showing you a spinner and a made-up number.

Signals are a starting point, not a promise. This method has no proven edge — check the record and decide for yourself.

Short trades are high risk. A signal cannot know the next candle.

This page reads the real market price. Pocket Option OTC prices are generated by Pocket Option and can differ from the real market — your trade settles on their price, not this one.

KES/USD is listed as unavailable: its real-market feed is too thin (about 20 candles a day) to compute these indicators honestly, so the scanner will not guess on it.
